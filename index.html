Quotation pack

1. What we ask you to price

  • Electrical: distribution board(s), dedicated 230 V circuits for equipment, 400 V three-phase connections, work sockets, lighting (ceiling panels, bar lights, sign outlets, facade), switches, cabling, earthing.
  • Air conditioning and extraction: ducted indoor units, outdoor unit(s), refrigerant and condensate pipes, oven hood extraction duct and fan. Unit sizes to be set by heat-load calculation: please state whether you include it.
  • Plumbing and drainage: cold and hot water points, drains and floor drains, hose point, connections to sink, hand-wash basins (kitchen entrance and plonge) and dishwasher. The plonge is the end of the kitchen: dirty-ware table, second hand-wash basin, waste bins, next to the dishwasher.
  • IT: network rack, data points, Wi-Fi access points, CCTV cabling (cabling and points only).
  • First floor: staff bedroom and upper floor (to rent as a kitchen): sockets, lighting, air conditioning, extraction, water, gas point.
  • Please price the ground floor and first floor as separate lots.
  • Please give: unit price per point, total per lot, exclusions, lead time, validity of the offer and payment terms.
